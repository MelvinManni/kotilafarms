# Writing and naming

Plain verbs, sentence case, no filler. The farm's words, not software words.

## Name things as the farm does

| Say | Never |
| --- | --- |
| Set, Set 4 | batch, cycle, production run, flock ID |
| day-olds | chicks inventory, DOCs (except in a supplier's own label) |
| a bag of feed, bags | units, SKUs |
| sawdust | litter material |
| brooding (charcoal, kerosene, fuel) | heating costs |
| deaths, mortality | losses, attrition |
| buyer | customer, client |
| owed, balance | receivables, AR |
| money in / money out | inflows / outflows |
| farm overhead | unallocated, general |
| weigh 10 birds | record a sample measurement |

## Buttons and actions

A button says exactly what happens: *Log today*, *Save sale*, *Record a payment*, *Fill in Friday*, *Weigh 10 birds*, *Reconcile cash*. Confirmation afterwards repeats the verb in the past: *Sale saved*. Never *Submit*, *OK*, *Proceed*.

## Headlines state findings

Panel headlines on reading screens say what the numbers mean:

- *Set 4 is 10% under weight — and the gap is widening*
- *₦412,000 is owed by 3 buyers*
- *Finisher runs out in about 4 days*
- *Nothing to worry about this week.* (when nothing is wrong: one line, no invented concerns)

## The weekly review voice

Written like a competent farm manager's note, not a dashboard. Three to six short points, each ending in something to do:

> Deaths were 4 this week, up from 2. Three of them were in the corner by the drinkers, where wet litter was logged three days running. **Change the sawdust there and raise the drinker line a notch.**

## Errors and empty states

Errors say what is wrong and how to fix it, without apology: *Choose a Set or farm overhead before saving.* *Bags can't be empty — fill any two of bags, price per bag and total.*

Empty states say what the section is for and offer the next step: *No weights for Set 5 yet. Weigh at least 10 birds from different corners each week — it's the only way to see a growth problem while there's still time to fix it.* → **Weigh 10 birds**. Never *No data available*.

## Dates, times, people

- Dates: *Sat 26 Sep*, *26 September 2026* in reports. Relative for the last week: *yesterday*, *Friday*.
- Times: *6:40pm*, 12-hour, no space.
- People by first name in the UI (*Chinedu logged this*), full name in the audit trail and reports.
