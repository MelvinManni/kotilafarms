# Logos

From the Kotila Farms brand folder (Drive). Single-ink marks: `kotila-mark-green.svg` is inked `#478918` (green-500), `kotila-mark-white.svg` is white for green-700 / green-900 grounds. `kotila-icon.svg` is the full-colour icon (green-500 stroke, yellow-500 fish, green-300 fields).

- Pair the icon with the Signika wordmark set as text: **Kotila** (600) **Farms** (300, green-700). Minimum icon width 32px.
- Clear space: the height of the fish's eye × 4 on every side.
- Don't recolour the full-colour icon, add effects, or place it on yellow.
- The white mark at 9–10% opacity, oversized and cropped, is the only background pattern allowed, and only on brand surfaces.
